# The owner's taste

Rules learned from the owner's feedback. They override craft.md. Add each new point with the date and
the video that taught it; never delete one without asking.

## Standing judgement (2026-11-19)

After two auto-edited reels (a lowrider parade, a Halloween night): "technically proficient at making the
content and using the tool, but missing taste, direction, attention to detail on what is in frame,
mistakes where the panning and zooming happens, and lack of touch."

What that meant in those two reels, as rules:

1. **Never let the auto-edit choose the photos.** Every shot is chosen from the casting sheet.
2. **Never open on a weak photo.** The Halloween reel opened on 1.5 s of a dark, soft arm.
3. **Look at what is in frame at a size where it can be judged** (540 px), at every cut and through
   every move. 180 px thumbnails hid soft focus and cut-off subjects.
4. **A cut-off title is a defect even mid-move.** Both reels had titles running off the frame edge.
5. **Deliver what was promised.** The "spooky spiral" layout was never seen whole.
6. **Touch: vary the holds.** Shots were nearly all 0.9 s; nothing was allowed to breathe.
